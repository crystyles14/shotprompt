ShotPrompt - version history
(Versions use major.minor.patch from v1.6.0. Older versions are listed as they were named.)

v1.14.0 (2026-10-16)
Design system 2.0: one look for the whole site, checked with a design review and a WCAG 2.1 AA
accessibility check.
- One set of colors, type, corners and buttons for all four builders and the home page. Every
  choice you tap is the same flat "key": grey when off, dark when on. Orange is only used for
  the main action (Copy, Draw). The yellow highlights and the heavy outlined buttons are gone.
- Contrast: grey text and the orange Copy button were slightly too light. Both now pass WCAG AA
  (4.5:1) in light and dark mode. Text boxes have stronger borders so they are easy to see.
- Touch: buttons and keys are at least 44 px tall on phones and tablets, and the small clear (x)
  buttons have a bigger tap area.
- Mobile: the top bar no longer hides Script and Storyboard on phones. The links move to a
  second row you can swipe.
- Keyboard: every control shows the same clear focus ring. The storyboard page has a page
  heading for screen readers.
- Home page redesigned: a camera viewfinder hero shows words being tapped and a prompt writing
  itself, for an image, a video and a storyboard frame. It plays twice, then stops. Two buttons
  to start right away. Builders shown as four cards with simple icons. Version and a link to
  this list in the footer.
- Motion, only where it answers what you did: keys settle when chosen, the next step slides in
  when you press Proceed, open sections fold out, the progress bar glides, the prompt box
  outlines when it changes, and Copy shows "Copied". The scroll-in effects on every section
  were removed so the page feels calmer. "Reduce motion" turns all of it off.
- Storyboard builder: buttons, view switcher, fields and selected frame now match the other
  builders, in light and dark mode.
- Updated the Image builder (2.2.0) and Video builder (2.1.0) to their newest versions.
- Script builder (1.2.0) and Storyboard builder (1.0.0): no changes.

v1.13.0 (2026-10-16)
- New builder: Storyboards (Storyboard builder 1.0.0). Plan a video frame by frame: sketch on
  each frame, add camera and story notes, play the board back, print or save as PDF, and get
  image and video prompts for every shot. Added to the home page and the top bar.
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.40.0): more models
  (Qwen Image 2.1, Dola AI (Seedream), Higgsfield (Seedream 4), Nano Banana, GPT Image,
  FLUX.2) and a new "Create a character" mode.
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 2.0.0): more models
  (MiniMax H3, Seedance 2.5 and 2.0, Wan 3.0, and LTX 2.5 and 2.3 in Advanced mode), a
  Simple and Advanced mode, and new video types (a place only, replace the background).
- Removed the "Where will you use this prompt?" switch. Both builders now write prompts for
  many models themselves, so the switch is no longer needed.
- Home page: four builder cards, and "Where your prompts work" now lists every supported model.
- Script builder (1.2.0): checked, no changes.

v1.12.0 (2026-10-14)
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.25.0): a model choice,
  Qwen Image 2.1 (ComfyUI) or Dola AI (Seedream), each with its own prompt style, and a
  step-by-step layout.
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.24.0): a model choice,
  MiniMax H3, LTX 2.5 or LTX 2.3, each with its own prompt style, a step-by-step layout,
  and prompts that follow MiniMax's official prompt guides.
- "Where will you use this prompt?" now only shows for MiniMax H3 and Qwen Image, because the
  other models already get plain prompts.
- "Other apps" format for video: works with the new MiniMax H3 prompt style (no empty lines
  for characters without a description, "N/A" music becomes "No background music").
- Builder pages: removed the "For MiniMax H3." and "For Qwen Image 2.1." at the start of the
  description, because each builder now supports more than one model.
- Home page: the cards and "Where your prompts work" list the new models.
- Script builder (1.2.0): checked, no changes since v1.9.0.

v1.11.0 (2026-10-10)
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.17.0): "Start from a preset"
  (for example Classic portrait, Beauty close-up, Fashion editorial, Street portrait) and a
  new "Photo quality: camera or film" choice.
- "Other apps" format for images: a third reference image now becomes "the third image".
- Script builder (1.2.0) and Video builder (1.15.0): checked, no changes since v1.10.0.

v1.10.0 (2026-10-10)
- Animation, following Dieter Rams' rule "less, but better": motion is short, calm, and only
  used where it explains something.
- Home page: each builder's frame shows how the site works. The word chips are tapped one by
  one, then the prompt (or script) writes itself. It plays twice, then stops.
- Home page: the frame corners move in like a camera finding focus, and sections fade in
  gently as you scroll.
- All pages: the orange dot next to the logo "powers on" when the page opens.
- All builders: the Copy button shows a check mark and "Copied" for a moment.
- All builders: the prompt box gets a short outline when it changes, so you can see what your
  choice did. Not shown while you are typing in the box yourself.
- All builders: buttons and word chips press down slightly, like real buttons.
- Everything respects the "reduce motion" setting on phones and computers. With it on, the site
  shows no animation and everything is visible right away.
- Builders: no changes since v1.9.0.

v1.9.0 (2026-10-10)
- Updated the Script builder to its newest version (Script builder 1.2.0): a new wide layout
  with three columns on big screens (tools on the left, the script in the middle like a page,
  save, downloads, vocabulary and format guide on the right). Download buttons are now in
  their own "Save and export" panel.
- Script page: the top bar now spans the same width as the new wide layout.
- Image builder (1.14.0) and Video builder (1.15.0): no changes since v1.8.0.

v1.8.0 (2026-10-10)
- New builder: Scripts and shotlists (Script builder 1.1.0). Add characters and scenes and get
  a script in real screenplay format, a Fountain version, and a shotlist. Download as PDF or
  Word. Added to the home page and the top bar ("Script").
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.14.0): you can say if you
  will use reference images, a theme list, and a short text that fits the theme. The prompt
  box is now editable.
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.15.0): three MiniMax H3
  prompt formats (Reference, Text-to-video storyboard, Image-to-video first and last frame),
  a theme list, and an editable prompt box.
- The "Prompt format" switch is now called "Where will you use this prompt?" with the choices
  "MiniMax H3" or "Qwen Image", and "Other apps". This avoids confusion with the video
  builder's own format choice. It now works with the editable prompt box, and your own edits
  are kept.
- Home page: the "Where your prompts work" section explains that the storyboard and first and
  last frame formats also work in many other apps.

v1.7.0 (2026-10-10)
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.9.1): new word groups for
  animals, sea life, insects, plants and flowers, food and drink, vehicles, buildings,
  fantasy creatures, objects and symbols, textures, subject actions and facing direction.
  The word count now changes color (good, okay, too short).
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.10): each character can
  now be a person, an animal or creature, or a product or object, with matching word lists
  (animal actions, product moves, facing, texture). Only people get spoken lines.
- Both builders: a small x button inside each box to clear it quickly.
- Simple format: now also works for animal and product subjects.
- Design: the clear (x) buttons follow the ShotPrompt colors, in light and dark mode.
- Home page: the Video prompts card now mentions people, animals and products.

v1.6.0 (2026-10-10)
- New "Prompt format" switch in both builders, above the prompt:
  - Image builder: "Qwen Image" (default) or "Simple (other apps)". Simple changes the
    <image1> / <image2> tags into plain words.
  - Video builder: "MiniMax H3" (default) or "Simple (other apps)". Simple removes the
    MiniMax H3 tags and fields and writes plain sentences: who the character is, what
    happens, what they say, the sound and the music.
  - Each choice shows a short note about where the prompt works. Your choice is remembered.
- Home page: new "Where your prompts work" section with what works best, what works with the
  Simple format, and the limits of each builder.
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.8): a pronoun choice for
  each character (she, he, they, it), so the word lists match the character.

v1.5 (2026-10-10)
- Home page: new "Made for" section describing who the site is for: creators short on
  time, filmmakers, and non-native English speakers.
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.8): many new word groups,
  such as color treatment, genre, pose, clothing cut, fabric, pattern, props, skin detail,
  backdrops, art movements and light setups. More image shapes (1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2, 2:3,
  4:5, 16:9, 9:16).
- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.7): a "Visual effects"
  box for every shot, and a new VFX tab in the word list (light and glow, particles,
  slow motion, focus, color looks, transitions, fantasy, glitch).

v1.4 (2026-10-10)
- Updated the Image builder to its newest version (Image builder 1.5): saved characters,

- Updated the Video builder to its newest version (Video builder 1.4).
- Characters saved in one builder now also appear in the other.
- Fixed the image shape buttons: they now show the full ratio (for example "Square 1:1"
  instead of "Square 1").
- Both builders use the ShotPrompt design, the Maya example character, and "MiniMax H3".
